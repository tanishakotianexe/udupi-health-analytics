05 / What we recommend

Simple actions based on what the graphs show.

Recommendation 1

Add beds where they are few

The map and share graph show most beds in a few big hospitals. Add beds in taluks with many centres but few beds.

Recommendation 2

Strengthen government hospitals

Private hospitals hold most of the beds. Upgrade the larger government hospitals so care stays affordable nearby.

Recommendation 3

Bring routine care back

Hospital stays are still below pre-COVID levels. Run check-up and awareness drives to bring patients back.

Recommendation 4

Prepare for dengue

Dengue admissions swing the most from year to year. Keep beds, tests and mosquito control ready before the season.

Recommendation 5

Plan for long-term illness

Diabetes and blood pressure visits keep rising. Add more regular check-up and medicine services.

Recommendation 6

Record causes of death

Many adult deaths have no cause noted. Better records will make every graph more reliable.

Where to start

Add beds where they are few, and bring routine hospital care back to normal.
