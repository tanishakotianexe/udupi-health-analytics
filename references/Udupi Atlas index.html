01 / Jargon Buster

What do PHC, CHC & GH mean?

Simple meanings of hospital short-forms used on the map.

🟢 PHC · 6 Beds

PHC = Village Clinic

Primary Health Centre: Small local clinic for fever, vaccines, and pregnancy check-ups. First stop for villagers.

🟢 UPHC · 4 Beds

UPHC = Town Clinic

Urban Primary Health Centre: Small city clinic (like in Malpe & Udupi town) for daily check-ups and basic medicine.

🟠 CHC · 30–50 Beds

CHC = Medium Block Hospital

Community Health Centre: Bigger than a PHC. Has specialist doctors, X-ray, and 24×7 baby delivery rooms.

🌲 GH · 100 Beds

GH = Taluk General Hospital

Main Town Govt Hospital: Located in Kundapura & Karkala towns. Handles surgeries, accidents, and emergencies.

🌲 District Hospital · 200–250 Beds

District & Mother–Child

Top Govt Hospitals in Udupi City: Ajjarkad Hospital (250 beds) & Mother-Child Hospital (200 beds). Free ICU & surgery.

🟠 Private · 80–2,032 Beds

Private Multi-Speciality

Big Private Hospitals: Led by KMC Manipal (2,032 beds), Adarsha, Mitra & TMA Pai for ICU and major surgery.

03 / Simple Takeaways

What we learned from the visualizations

Four short, plain-English facts from the maps and charts.

Takeaway 1 · Bed Concentration

76% of all beds are in Udupi Taluk

Out of 4,268 beds in the district, 3,256 beds are in Udupi & Manipal alone (KMC Manipal has 2,032 beds). Forest taluks like Hebri have only 42 beds total.

Takeaway 2 · Govt vs. Private

Villages rely 100% on Govt clinics

Private hospitals (3,032 beds) are only in 4 main towns. In rural villages, Government PHCs and CHCs (1,236 beds across 71 centres) are the only option.

Takeaway 3 · 5 km Travel Reach

Coastal highway is covered; hills have gaps

Communities along the NH-66 coastal road have a clinic within 5 km. Forest and hill villages near the Western Ghats (Hebri, Byndoor, eastern Kundapura) must travel 10–20 km.

Takeaway 4 · Clinic Hierarchy

Good first-aid, long travel for emergencies

Villagers can easily get vaccines and fever medicine at a 6-bed PHC, but snakebites, heart attacks, or C-section deliveries require a long trip to town.

04 / What To Do Next

Recommendations, Future Scope & Conclusion

Clear, simple action points based on the maps.

Recommendations

How to improve care in Udupi

  • 1. Add more beds in Hebri, Byndoor & Nitte: Upgrade 30-bed CHCs into 50–100 bed hospitals with 24×7 surgery and blood banks.
  • 2. Mobile clinic vans for hill villages: Send regular doctor vans to forest villages inside the red dashed Hill Gap Zones.
  • 3. Video-link village PHCs to Manipal: Let rural PHC doctors consult KMC Manipal & District Hospital specialists online.
Future Scope

What we can add to this website later

  • 1. Real road travel time: Show 15-minute and 30-minute ambulance driving times instead of straight-line circles.
  • 2. Population per clinic: Add village population counts to see how many people share each hospital bed.
  • 3. Live doctor & ICU count: Show how many doctors, nurses, and ICU ventilators are available at each hospital.
Final Conclusion

Strong healthcare overall, but rural hospitals need more beds

Udupi has a strong network of 80 health centres and 4,268 beds. We do not need to build dozens of new buildings—instead, adding beds, emergency doctors, and ambulances to existing rural CHCs in Hebri, Byndoor, Kundapura, and Karkala will make healthcare fair for everyone.
